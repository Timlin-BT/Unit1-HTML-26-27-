Tomatoes and Fried egg

I picked Tomatoes and Fried egg because it is a traditional dish for where I am from. as a child, my parents always made it for me, engraving the arouma into my mind.
Overtime there was no way that I couldn't remember the recipe.

Ingredients

  • 2 large Tomatoes
  • 4 large eggs
  • Salt
  • Oil (of your choice)
  • Msg
  • Spring onions

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them to a even consistency.
  2. Cut and wash the Tomatoes into semi-equal pieces to prepare for cooking.
  3. Pour oil into the pan with your desired cooking oil.
  4. As the pan warms, put the tomatoes into the pan and cook till partially soft.
  5. Put the tomatoes to the side and proceed cook the eggs half way to your desired wellness.
  6. When you reach that point put in and adjust your seasonings till done.
  7. Plate and put cut spring onions ontop.

Notes

Equipment needed:

  • Pan, spatula, electric or regular stove, whisk, and a plate to fit the amount of servings created.
    • Substitutions:Large wok/wok (for pan), Spoon, chopsticks, or anyother eating utensil (For whisk and/or spatula)
  • Nutrition facts: 450 calories, 27.4 grams or protein, 33.7 grams of fat, and 11 grams of carbs.
    • (For double protein [8 eggs]) 620 calories, 52.6 grams of protein, 38.8 grams of fat, and 12.8 grams of carbs.
